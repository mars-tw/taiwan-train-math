To experience the new math mechanic in under three minutes from a fresh install:

1. On the first screen, tap **我來調度** in **拆合車廂調度台** (Carriage Math Dispatch).
2. Tap **知道了，先裝滿列車**. Each dot represents one passenger. Tap **上車 1 人** five times, then **人數好了，去分車**.
3. Tap **移到後車 1 人**, then **分好了，車廂分開**. Moving a passenger changes each carriage's quantity while preserving the total: 4 + 1 = 5.
4. The rear carriage stays at the station. Select **4 人** to complete the subtraction task. The completion screen connects 4 + 1 = 5 with 5 − 1 = 4. Wrong answers allow unlimited retries.
5. Tap **暫停／返回**, close and reopen the app. The home card retains the completed count; an unfinished dispatch resumes its exact quantities. **再開一班** starts a new quantity combination. Existing progress may show a later combination, so follow the quantities displayed on screen.

The learning objective is number composition and quantity conservation. Island Transport Academy focuses on vehicle control and transport activities.
